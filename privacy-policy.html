Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 July 2026

Bora helps you and your friends see when you're all free and plan get-togethers. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, and the control you have over it.

The short version: Bora reads only your busy/free times — never your event titles, locations, or guests. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time.
What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account — your email address, used to sign you in and secure your account.
  • Profile — a display name, and an optional profile photo. Your profile photo is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Availability & plans — the free-time windows you set, the plans you create (title, time, and category), and the friends you connect with in the app.
Calendars

If you connect a calendar

Connecting your Apple or Google calendar is optional and off until you turn it on. When you do, Bora reads only your busy and free times to help pre-fill your availability. It never reads or stores the titles, locations, guests, or notes of your events.

  • Apple Calendar — read on your device through iOS. Nothing about your events leaves your phone except the availability windows you choose to share with your friends.
  • Google Calendar — connected through Google's secure sign-in. Bora requests only the "free/busy" permission, and your Google access can be revoked at any time in Bora's Settings or your Google account.
Who we share with

Services that help run Bora

We use a small number of trusted providers to operate the app. We share only what each one needs to do its job.

Supabase

Hosts our database and handles sign-in. Stores your account, availability, plans, and friend connections.

Anthropic

Powers the AI place suggestions. When you ask for ideas, the city and any preferences you type are sent to generate recommendations.

Mollie

Creates the payment links for splitting a bill. Only the amount and a short description are sent — Bora never sees or stores card or bank details.

Google

Only if you connect Google Calendar, and only for free/busy access as described above.

Your control

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your account, availability, plans, and friend connections from our servers — immediately and irreversibly.

Under the GDPR you also have the right to access, correct, or export your personal data. Email us and we'll help.

Security & scope

How we protect it

  • Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers.
  • Any calendar access tokens are stored securely in your device's Keychain, not on our servers.
  • Bora is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.
Changes

Updates to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app. Continued use of Bora after an update means you accept the revised policy.
