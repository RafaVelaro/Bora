Common questions

Frequently asked

How do I see when my friends are free?

Add friends with an invite code (Friends tab), mark the days you're available on the Availability tab, then open Find Time and pick who you want to meet — Bora shows the windows when you're all free.

How do I connect my calendar?

Open the account menu (person icon in Friends) → Settings. You can connect Apple Calendar and Google Calendar separately — turn on either, both, or neither. Bora reads only your busy/free times to pre-fill your availability, never your event details.

How does the AI spot suggestion work?

When you create a plan, tap "Need ideas?" (or "Find the perfect spot"), enter the city and any preferences, and Bora suggests venues that fit your group — each with quick links to Google Maps and Instagram.

How do I split the bill?

Open a plan, enter the total, and choose who's chipping in. Bora shows the per-person amount and can create an iDEAL payment link (via Mollie) you can share — friends pay from their own bank, no app required.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the person icon in the Friends tab → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all your data, immediately and irreversibly.

Is my data private?

Yes. Bora reads only busy/free times — never event titles, locations, or guests — and we never sell your data or show ads. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
